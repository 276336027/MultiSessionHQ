# MultiSessionHQ — Instant Account Switching for Chrome

Switch between your accounts in one click — no logging out, no
re-entering passwords, no lost sessions.

## The problem
If you manage multiple store accounts, client profiles, or work
logins, you know the pain: logging out and back in all day,
2FA every time, sessions lost when you switch.

## How it works
1. Log in once — click Save to snapshot the session
2. Switch accounts with a single click
3. Every session stays alive, switch back anytime

## Features
- 📸 Full session snapshots: cookies (incl. HttpOnly & subdomains) +
  LocalStorage/SessionStorage
- ⚡ One-click switching, unlimited parallel sessions
- 🗂 Organized by site, with custom labels & notes
- 📤 Export / Import: JSON (Cookie-Editor compatible), cookies.txt
  (Netscape), header strings, and Playwright storageState
- 💾 Full backup: export everything, restore on any machine
- 🔒 100% local — your data never leaves your browser. No servers,
  no tracking, no accounts required.

## Who is it for?
E-commerce sellers, social media managers, agencies, and QA
engineers who juggle many accounts daily.
